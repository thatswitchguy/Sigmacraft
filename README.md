This is a small Minecraft-inspired game with not much to it

<ins>**Consists of:**</ins>

Inventory
Developer mode (accessible with password)
Crafting (currently doesnt give you the output)
Trees 
Small world
OK FPS (avg.60 on a good enough computer) (~20-25 on school chromebooks)
Small render distance to minimise lag

I will try to get crafting working, and you can fork this here ➤ [<kbd>**Replit fork link**</kbd>](https://replit.com/@zakariya2013/Sigmacraft)

<ins>**Basic Controls:**</ins>

Mine: Left click
Place: Right click
Pause menu: esc
Inventory: E
Move item: click once on item and move cursor and then click again to place down
Seperate item from a stack: Shift+click while holding item
Hotbar: 1-9 for slots
